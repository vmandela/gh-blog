---
title: SystemC with Bazel
---

I have started playing with [SystemC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SystemC) for a work assignment.
I first heard about SystemC in 2005 and been using SystemC simulators since 2018. 
This is the first time I would be doing modelling using SystemC.

The ecosystem seems to be strongly tied to CMake as the build system.
I am stuck with the idea that bazel is the better build system if the models have to scale
across multiple devices.
Its time to prototype quickly with bazel before it is too late.
